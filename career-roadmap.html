📍 Your Personal Blueprint

2-Year Career Roadmap

Built around your skills, your schedule, and your goals. 10–20 hrs/week. Tight budget. Big ambitions. Let's go.

Timeline 24 Months
Weekly Hours 10–20 hrs
Target Income $5K–$15K/mo
End Goal Your Own Agency
1
Phase 1 Months 1–3 · Stabilize Income
Get Cash Flowing 💵
Your A+ cert + basic web skills = enough to land remote IT support work RIGHT NOW. This is your financial bridge. Don't overthink it — get the income, then build the dream.
🖥️
Apply for Remote IT Helpdesk Roles
Target Indeed, LinkedIn, and Remote.co. Filter: "remote helpdesk", "IT support part-time". Your A+ cert is your golden ticket — mention it in every application headline.
Week 1–2
📁
Set Up a Simple Portfolio Site
Use what you already know — HTML, CSS, JS. One page. Your name, your skills, 2–3 past projects, and a contact form. This is your storefront.
Week 2–3
🎯
Take 1–2 Freelance Web Projects
Friends, family, local businesses — anyone who needs a website. Charge $300–$800. This builds your portfolio AND starts your freelance reputation. Don't wait for it to be perfect.
Month 2–3
Est. Monthly Income
$800–$2K
2
Phase 2 Months 3–9 · Build the Brand
Become the Go-To Designer 🎨
Now that income isn't suffocating you, it's time to build your freelance reputation. Pick a niche. Get serious about Figma. Start getting referrals. This is where your identity as a designer/developer takes shape.
🎯
Pick a Niche & Stick to It
Restaurants, real estate agents, local service businesses, gyms — pick ONE. Specialists charge more and get hired faster than generalists. "I build websites for restaurants" beats "I build websites" every time.
Month 3
✏️
Level Up Figma Skills
Spend 2 hrs/week on Figma practice. Learn components, auto layout, and prototyping. Free resource: Figma's own YouTube channel + "Figma for Beginners" on their site. This separates you from developers who can't design.
Month 3–5
💼
Join Upwork + Contra
Create profiles on Upwork AND Contra (freelancer-friendly, no fees). Apply to 3–5 jobs per week. Your first few gigs may be lower pay — that's fine. Reviews > money at this stage.
Month 4
📣
Start Posting on LinkedIn
Post 2x per week. Share work-in-progress, before/afters, lessons learned. You don't need to go viral — you need to be visible when someone Googles you or checks your profile after a referral.
Month 4–9
Est. Monthly Income
$2K–$4K
3
Phase 3 Months 9–18 · Launch the Agency
Stop Freelancing, Start Owning 🏗️
This is the jump from "person who does work" to "business that delivers results." You create a business name, simple packages, and start thinking about recurring income — not just one-off projects.
🏢
Form Your LLC
Use ZenBusiness or Northwest Registered Agent (~$50–$150/year). This legitimizes you, protects your personal assets, and lets clients pay your business — not you personally. Huge credibility boost.
Month 9
📦
Create Service Packages
Starter ($1,200), Growth ($2,500), Premium ($5,000+). Include maintenance retainers ($150–$300/mo) in every package. Retainers = predictable monthly income without hunting for new clients every month.
Month 9–10
🤝
Build a Referral System
Offer past clients $100–$200 for every referral that closes. Partner with local marketing agencies, accountants, and business coaches — they have clients who need websites and they'll send them your way.
Month 10–12
🤖
Add AI Integration as an Upsell
Learn to add AI chatbots (using OpenAI API or Voiceflow) to client websites. Charge $500–$1,500 as an add-on. Businesses LOVE this — it feels futuristic to them and it's a skill most web designers don't have.
Month 12–15
Est. Monthly Income
$4K–$8K
4
Phase 4 Months 18–24 · Scale & Future-Proof
Build the Machine 🚀
You've got clients, income, and a process. Now you stop doing everything yourself and start building systems. Subcontract work, add passive income streams, and cement yourself as the go-to expert in your niche.
👥
Hire a Subcontractor
Find a junior developer on Upwork or Discord communities. Pay them $20–$35/hr, charge your client $75–$100/hr. You manage, they execute. This is how a solo freelancer becomes an agency owner.
Month 18
🎓
Launch a Mini Course or Digital Product
Teach what you've learned — "How I built a web design agency with no degree" or "Web Design for [Your Niche]." Sell on Gumroad for $27–$97. This is income that comes in while you sleep and builds your authority.
Month 20
🌐
Deepen AI Specialization
By this point, AI tools will have evolved. Stay ahead by learning the latest — whether that's AI-generated websites, automation workflows, or custom GPTs for clients. This keeps you irreplaceable in a world where basic web work gets commoditized.
Month 18–24
Est. Monthly Income
$8K–$15K+

This isn't a straight line — there will be slow months, bad clients, and moments of doubt. That's not failure, that's the process. Every agency owner you admire went through the exact same thing.

You've already got the skills. Now it's just execution. 💪
